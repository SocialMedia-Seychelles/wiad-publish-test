Good to know

How far is the beach?

A ten-minute walk down the hill path, or two minutes by car. We'll show you the shortcut.

Is it quiet?

Very. Four rooms, no pool party, birds in the morning and waves at night.

Do I need a car?

Not for the beach. For the rest of the island, a bus stops at the bottom of the lane and we can arrange a hire car.

How do I pay?

We confirm your room on WhatsApp and send a secure payment link for the deposit.
